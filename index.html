Core Data Products

📍 SweetSpot.io

Urban Spatial Optimization Engine

The Challenge: Home buyers face massive information overload when trying to balance subjective lifestyle preferences—such as transit access, walkability constraints, and hyper-local safety parameters—across completely disconnected municipal open datasets.

Product Overview: A "Life-Centric Real Estate Match Engine." It standardizes unlinked public transit maps, walkability scores, and safety data into a centralized multi-objective optimization formula. Through an interactive heatmap interface, users can manipulate slider weights based on their unique priorities to immediately isolate their perfect geographic block. If you are unsure just highlight a geographic area on the map by zipcode/city to verify avg living expenses, school district ratings, employment opportunity scores and commute times to explore and find that sweet neighborhood spot you would love to settle.

🥗 Verified Bites

Predictive Restaurant Compliance & Transparency Platform

The Challenge: While popular crowdsourced dining applications excel at displaying user food reviews, they routinely obscure critical health, safety, and sanitation histories deep inside outdated, hard-to-navigate county government databases.

Product Overview: A consumer transparency platform that reconciles highly asymmetric municipal health inspector records with live commercial location APIs. It translates raw, disparate inspection outcomes into a standardized, scannable 0–100 Sanitation Risk Index (SRI). By applying an exponential time-decay function, recent critical violations heavily spike active visibility, while older historical infractions seamlessly trend toward zero risk.
