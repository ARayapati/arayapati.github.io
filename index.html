Core Data Products

📍 SweetSpot.io

Urban Spatial Optimization Engine

The Challenge: Home buyers face massive information overload when trying to balance subjective lifestyle preferences—such as transit access, walkability constraints, and hyper-local safety parameters—across completely disconnected municipal open datasets.

Product Overview: A "Life-Centric Real Estate Match Engine." It standardizes unlinked public transit maps, walkability scores, and safety data into a centralized multi-objective optimization formula. Through an interactive heatmap interface, users can manipulate slider weights based on unique priorities to isolate their target geographic block.

🥗 Verified Bites

Predictive Restaurant Compliance & Transparency Platform

The Challenge: While crowdsourced dining apps excel at displaying user reviews, they obscure critical health, safety, and sanitation histories deep inside hard-to-navigate county government databases.

Product Overview: A consumer transparency platform that reconciles asymmetric municipal health inspector records with live commercial location APIs. Translates raw inspection outcomes into a standardized, scannable 0–100 Sanitation Risk Index (SRI) using an exponential time-decay function to dynamically weight violation recency.
